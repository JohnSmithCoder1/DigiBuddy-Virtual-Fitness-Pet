What if your step tracker actually cared if you showed up today?

DigiBuddy turns your daily steps into care for a virtual pet who depends on you. Your steps are their walks, and every day you move helps keep your digi happy. It’s a fun, motivating layer that transforms step tracking from something you forget into something you genuinely look forward to every day.

Why you’ll love DigiBuddy:
* A fitness buddy who needs you — Your digi reacts to your real-world steps, celebrating your wins and gently letting you know when they need a little more from you
* Step goals made personal — Choose a daily step goal that works for you, earn Happy Days when you reach it, and level up your digi as you keep showing up
* Automatic Apple Health sync — DigiBuddy automatically syncs your steps with Apple Health, so there’s nothing extra to log or keep track of
* Your digi goes where you go — Keep up with DigiBuddy from Home Screen widgets on your iPhone and the companion app on Apple Watch
* Smart, supportive notifications — Friendly reminders automatically stop once you’ve hit your goal for the day
* Encouragement over pressure — DigiBuddy is built around motivation and consistency. Every day is a fresh start, and your digi is always rooting for you.

If you struggle to stay consistent or lose interest in traditional step trackers after a week, your digi is waiting.
Download DigiBuddy and make every step mean a little more.
